Verification-Gated Local Model Cascading

An oracle-gated study of task-level complementarity among three local Qwen2.5-Coder tiers on 542 EvalPlus tasks. The findings describe this workload and evaluation procedure.

61.07%Adaptive · 331 / 542
54.80%Strong-only · 297 / 542
40.59%Small-only · 220 / 542

Adaptive retained 34 passes not present in Strong-only (+6.27 percentage points) on this fixed workload. This does not establish better performance under equal budgets or in general use.

Interpretation

Evidence and limits

The result supports non-nested pass sets under oracle test gating and task-level complementarity in this fixed workload.

The same EvalPlus Plus result controlled escalation and defined the primary endpoint. The experiment does not establish independent verification, predictive routing, equal-budget performance, or universal efficiency.

It used one task order and seed, one hardware configuration, the Qwen2.5-Coder family, and Q4_K_M quantization.
